Frequently asked questions

Supplement facts and routine facts, in plain language — not marketing copy.

Routine
What happens if I miss a day?

Less than you'd think. Supplements work through consistent use over time — one or two missed days won't meaningfully set you back, and there's no need to double up the next day to "catch up." Just pick back up.

Facts
Do I need a multivitamin if I already eat well?

Often, yes — it's genuinely difficult to close every nutrient gap through food alone, even with a good diet. A multivitamin is best thought of as a baseline, not a replacement for eating well.

Routine
Does it matter what time of day I take my supplements?

For some, yes. Energizing ones (like B-vitamins) tend to work better earlier in the day; others (like magnesium) can help with winding down at night. Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) absorb better with a meal that has some fat in it.

Facts
Can supplements interact badly with each other?

Some can — calcium and iron, for example, compete for absorption if taken together. Spacing them a few hours apart helps your body use both properly. Re's Insights tab flags known interactions automatically.

Routine
How long before I actually notice a difference?

Varies a lot by supplement — some (like caffeine-adjacent energy support) are near-immediate, others (like omega-3s or vitamin D) can take 4–8 weeks of consistent use before effects are noticeable. This is exactly why consistency matters more than any single day.

Re
Why doesn't a missed day reset my streak to zero?

Because that's not how habits actually work, and punishing a bad week is how people quit for good. Re keeps the count, but it's information — never a reason to feel like you're starting over.
